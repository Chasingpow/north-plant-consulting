What We Do

Seven capabilities that make a plant deliver

Hands-on operations consulting for manufacturers — grounded in the Toyota Production System and focused on measurable improvement, not binders on a shelf.

Operational Assessment

A structured on-site review of flow, scheduling, staffing, data, and daily management — showing exactly where your plant loses time and money, with a prioritized improvement roadmap.

Production Control & Scheduling

Constraint-based master scheduling, order launch and release rules, and real-time order visibility — so every department knows what to deliver, in what sequence, by when.

Process Data Capture

The shop-floor data most plants never collect — cycle times, miss reasons, order aging, downtime causes — captured simply and reliably, so decisions run on facts instead of anecdotes.

Dashboards & Real-Time Visibility

Where every order sits, which department holds it, and how long it's been there — live plant dashboards and hour-by-hour boards the whole floor can see and act on.

KPI Systems & Accountability

Metric definitions everyone agrees on — OTD, first-pass yield, productivity per labour hour — with cascaded objectives and tiered daily management that turn numbers into ownership.

Org Design & Capacity Planning

Floor org charts with defined spans of control, job descriptions rebuilt against real work, headcount models tied to demand, and skills matrices so cross-coverage is planned — not improvised.

Training, SOPs & Standard Work

Standardized work for every core job, TWI-style train-the-trainer programs, tiered operator certification with visible tracking, and hazard analysis built into every SOP.

Who We Are

Toyota discipline, applied to your floor

North Plant Consulting is built on Toyota Production System experience — standardized work, TWI-style job instruction, tiered daily management, and visual control, applied hands-on in real plants, not taught from a slide deck.

Our approach is system-first: when delivery slips or a department is drowning, the root cause is usually a missing process, not a missing person. We fix the system, then build the roles, training, and cadence that keep it running after we leave.
